Meet the Wonder Bean

Teacher by day, doodle enthusiast by heart, creator always.

Drawing is my happy place — one where I can escape the chaotic world that can sometimes feel like it is literally consuming my entire being.

Growing up in a family of teachers, I always wanted to set myself apart by creating. But in the years of doing teaching part-time and after-school care, I came to the conclusion: maybe I could do both?

🌱

The Teacher

Connecting with little hearts, instilling confidence, and inspiring curiosity.

🎨

The Creator

Embracing bold imagination, playful colors, and pure whimsical wonder.

Teaching for me has always involved so much more than just passing down knowledge. You have to engage your little audience, and inspire, and instill confidence, and fix behaviours, and be bigger than yourself. Something that boldness only art and creating art can teach you.

Anyway, enough about me! Here is my safe space, which I hope is just as fun and super whimsical as I intended. I am just more than thrilled that you have stumbled upon my little wonder bean creations — which you may be wondering what on earth is that — and that you should find yourself in my world. If I can even instill a drop of wonder, I reckon that would be some pretty cool beans.

♥

With love from, the Wonder Bean herself.
